工作英语 · 主动表达

你想表达：最近工作压力很大。

不要逐字翻译。先看一个自然英文结构，再用自己的真实情况写一句。

表达模板pattern
I've been under a lot of pressure at work lately.
为什么自然？结构解释

英语里通常说 people are busy 或 people are under pressure，而不是 work is busy。

可复用结构： I've been under a lot of pressure with/from ... lately.

用你的真实情况写一句

表达纠错 · 2/3
用户表达

My work is very busy recently.

AI 会先理解你的意图，再给出更自然的英文表达。

I understand. You mean you have a lot of work and feel stressed lately. A more natural way to say it is: I've been really busy with work lately.
模拟 Try Again
I've been under a lot of pressure at work lately because we have several deadlines.
表达纠错自然表达

My work is very busy recently.
I've been really busy with work lately.

Why: busy usually describes a person or schedule. “with work lately” sounds more natural than “work is busy recently”.
