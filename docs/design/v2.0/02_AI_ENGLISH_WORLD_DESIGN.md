# Lin Muen AI English World Design v2.0

## Vision

English Tutor Agent v2.0 is an immersive English learning world driven by Lin Muen IP.

Users do not only study lessons. They experience stories, complete missions, and improve English through realistic scenarios.

## World Structure

Season -> Episode -> Scene -> Learning Tasks

## Season 1: Getting Closer to English

1. Meet Lin Muen
2. Coffee Shop Morning
3. Weekend Shopping
4. Fashion Blogger Day
5. Preparing Singapore Trip
6. Airport Adventure
7. Hotel Check-in
8. Meeting Foreign Friends
9. Work Meeting
10. English Challenge

## Design Principle

Every story must combine:

- Character connection
- Real life scenario
- English learning objective
- Interactive practice

The content should feel like a story, not a textbook.
